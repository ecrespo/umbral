## Decisions worth knowing

- **Pragmas travel in the DSN**, not as statements after connecting. `database/sql` owns a pool, so a pragma issued once would apply to a single connection and silently not to the next one. `Open` then reads `foreign_keys` and `journal_mode` back rather than trusting the driver.
- **`Recover` takes the time as an argument** instead of calling `time.Now` itself, which is what lets the test assert the exact `exited_at` value in UTC epoch milliseconds (Art. 6).
- **Recovery is one transaction.** A crash halfway must not leave sessions exited while their blocks still claim to be running.
- Driver is `modernc.org/sqlite`: pure Go, no cgo, as the constitution's stack table requires.
