## What failed

The daemon's side held in every run: each `terminal_interact` call was decided and recorded,
every destructive ask was put to the user (run 7's `rm -fr .git/rebase-merge`, denied twice),
no turn hung past its cancel, and no container, temp directory or daemon was left behind.
The psql task passed in all ten runs. The rebase failed in all ten, on the model's use of vim,
not on the tool:

- **The cursor after `ciw`** (runs 2, 3). After `<Esc>`, vim leaves the cursor on the last
  letter of `squash`; the model's next `j ciw` lands on the commit hash, and git stops with
  `invalid line 3: pick squash # add three`.
- **`Esc` written as text** (runs 1, 5). A bare `"Esc"` is text by decision 1; only `<Esc>`, a
  key object or a lone ESC character is the key. vim stayed in insert mode and typed
  `squashEscjciwsquashEsc:wq` into the todo list.
- **An escape glued to text** (runs 4, 9). The model wrote `"\u001b:"`, an ESC followed by `:`,
  which decision 1 still refuses; its next steps were calls Ollama could not parse, and the
  repaired step failed again, ending the turn `tool_error` as REQ-AGT-006 says. Across the ten
  runs the daemon logged six unreadable calls asked again (runs 1, 3 twice, 4, 7, 9) and two
  turns ended after the repair (runs 4, 9).
- **Normal-mode commands that do not do what the model meant** (runs 6, 7, 8, 10): `squash`
  typed in normal mode, `s` and `4x` on the wrong column, `2G0dw`, and in runs 3 and 7 a loop of
  aborts and retries until the 10-minute limit.

## Residuals

- An ESC prefix inside text (`"\u001b:"`) is refused. Reading a leading ESC as `escape`
  followed by the text would have saved runs 4 and 9's first refusal; it is left for the Tech
  Lead, because it widens decision 1's "a control character inside text is refused".
- The model does not track vim's cursor across edits. A `read_screen` between edits would have
  shown it; the tool's description already says to read the screen, and the model did not.

Whether US-F2-002 passes is left to the Tech Lead, as T-F2-12's measurement was.
