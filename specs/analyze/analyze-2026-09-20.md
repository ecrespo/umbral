## Constitution

No violations.

- **Art. 4:** REQ-SEC-011 keeps remote updates disabled by default and logs every fetch in
  `egress_log`; offline operation is preserved. B-01 is about integrity, not about the principle.
- **Art. 5:** DD-012 keeps external reports away from permissions: they change what is displayed and
  awaited, never what a tool may do.
- **Art. 6 and 9:** the new tables follow the ID, time and money rules, and DD-015 adds a mechanical
  check against spec drift.
