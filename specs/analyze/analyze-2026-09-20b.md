## Constitution

No violations. The two amendments of 2026-09-20 are recorded in the amendment table with their
rationale, and both new requirements sit inside them:

- REQ-SEC-012 restricts the environment fallback to an unavailable keyring, explicit opt-in and a
  degraded state reported in `umb status`;
- REQ-SEC-011 and REQ-SEC-013 make the signature a condition for applying any network-delivered rule
  material, and REQ-SEC-016 keeps recovery working without network and without keys.
